  • Full name

  • Email address

  • Order and purchase details
  • Payment-related information provided through our payment partners

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to provide our products and services.

We may use your information to:

  • Process and confirm your orders

  • Process eligible exchanges
  • Handle complaints and queries

  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized transactions
  • Improve our products and customer experience
  • Communicate promotional offers where permitted

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to your interaction with Twinkal Apparel unless permitted or required by applicable law.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework requires personal-data processing to be handled with appropriate notice and lawful purposes, including requirements concerning consent where consent is the basis of processing.

3. Sharing of Information

Twinkal Apparel does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers where required to fulfill your order, including:

  • Courier and delivery partners
  • Payment gateway/service providers

  • Customer-support service providers

Only information reasonably necessary for the relevant service will be shared.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal proceedings, government authorities, or to protect our legal rights.

4. Payment Information

Where online payments are available, payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Twinkal Apparel does not intend to store sensitive payment credentials such as your complete card number, CVV or banking password on our own systems.

Customers should never share OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or banking passwords with anyone claiming to represent Twinkal Apparel.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration or disclosure.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies

If Twinkal Apparel operates a website, the website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Understand website usage
  • Remember certain preferences
  • Improve the customer experience

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings.

7. Marketing Communications

If you provide your contact information for promotional communication, we may occasionally send information about:

  • New collections
  • Offers
  • Discounts
  • Product launches
  • Other Twinkal Apparel updates

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

Return & Exchange Policy

TWINKAL APPAREL

Return & Exchange Policy

Effective Date: 16 August 2026

At Twinkal Apparel, we carefully inspect our products before dispatch and want you to have a smooth shopping experience.

We currently follow an Exchange-Only Policy.

1. Exchange Period

Exchange requests must be raised within 36 hours of delivery of the order.

Requests received after this period may not be accepted.

To request an exchange, please contact us through Instagram or email with:

  • Order number
  • Reason for exchange
  • Clear photographs of the product
  • Photograph of the product tag/label
  • Unboxing video, where applicable

2. When Can You Request an Exchange?

An exchange may be requested if:

  • Wrong Product Received
  • Manufacturing Defect
  • Product Damaged During Delivery
  • Size Exchange

3. Conditions for Exchange

The product must be:

  • Unworn
  • Unwashed
  • Unused
  • Unaltered
  • Free from stains, makeup, perfume or other marks
  • Returned with original tags attached
  • Returned with the original packaging, where applicable

Products that do not meet these conditions may be rejected for exchange.

4. Size Exchange

If you require a different size, we may provide an exchange subject to availability.

For products with limited inventory, the requested replacement size may not always be available.

If the required size is unavailable, we may offer another suitable resolution at our discretion.

Please check the size details carefully before placing your order.

5. Wrong or Defective Product

If Twinkal Apparel sends you the wrong product or the product has a verified manufacturing defect, we will arrange an eligible replacement/exchange in accordance with this policy.

If the replacement product is unavailable, we may provide an alternative resolution, which may include store credit or refund, as applicable.

6. Damaged Product

If your parcel appears damaged at the time of delivery, please document the condition of the package and contact us as soon as possible.

For damage claims, photographs and/or an unboxing video may be requested to help us verify the issue.

7. Unboxing Video

We strongly recommend recording a continuous unboxing video when opening your package.

An unboxing video can help us verify claims involving:

  • Wrong product
  • Missing items
  • Damage
  • Packing issues
  • Product defects

For certain claims, Twinkal Apparel may require an unboxing video or other evidence before approving an exchange.

8. Products Not Eligible for Exchange

Exchange requests will generally not be accepted for products that:

  • Have been worn
  • Have been washed
  • Have been altered or tailored
  • Are damaged due to customer handling
  • Have stains, makeup, perfume or other marks
  • Are missing original tags
  • Are returned without the required packaging
  • Are returned after the exchange period
  • Are being returned solely because the customer changed their mind

9. Colour Variation

Actual product colours may vary slightly from photographs because of:

  • Lighting conditions during photography
  • Camera settings
  • Mobile/computer screen settings
  • Individual display brightness and colour profiles

Minor colour variation will not normally be considered a product defect.

10. No Cash Refund for Change of Mind

Twinkal Apparel does not offer refunds for:

  • Change of mind
  • Personal preference
  • Colour preference
  • Incorrect size selection by the customer

Eligible customers may request an exchange in accordance with this policy.

11. Exchange Shipping

For a verified wrong, damaged or defective product, Twinkal Apparel will provide the applicable exchange solution.

For a customer-requested size exchange, applicable return/re-shipping charges may be borne by the customer.

Any applicable charges will be communicated before the exchange is processed.

12. How to Request an Exchange

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery:

Email: twinkalapparel@gmail.com

Instagram: Twinkal Apparel on Instagram

Please mention: Order Number + Exchange Reason + Product Photos

Our team will review your request and guide you through the exchange process.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

TWINKAL APPAREL

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: 16 August 2026

Twinkal Apparel delivers products across India through third-party courier and logistics partners.

1. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days after order confirmation.

Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays may be processed on the next working day.

2. Delivery Timeline

Estimated delivery time is generally: 3–7 business days.

Delivery timelines may vary depending on:

  • Delivery location
  • Courier availability
  • Weather conditions
  • Public holidays
  • Festivals
  • Operational disruptions
  • Other circumstances outside our control

Delivery timelines are estimates and not guaranteed.

3. Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for eligible locations and orders.

COD availability may vary depending on the delivery address and courier service.

Twinkal Apparel may contact customers to confirm COD orders before dispatch.

4. Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing the correct:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • House/building number
  • Street/locality
  • City
  • State
  • PIN code

If an order is returned because of an incorrect or incomplete address, customer unavailability, or refusal to accept the parcel, additional charges may apply if re-delivery is requested.

5. Delivery Attempts

Courier partners may make multiple delivery attempts according to their operational procedures.

Customers should remain available at the provided delivery address or coordinate with the courier where necessary.

6. Delayed Delivery

If your order is delayed beyond the expected delivery period, please contact Twinkal Apparel with your order details.

We will assist in checking the shipment status with the courier partner.

7. Package Damage

If your parcel appears visibly damaged when delivered, please take photographs/videos of the package before opening it and contact us as soon as possible.

8. Shipping Charges

Delivery charges may vary depending on the customer's delivery address/PIN code and applicable courier charges. The applicable shipping charges, if any, will be communicated to the customer before order confirmation. Any applicable COD charges will also be communicated before the order is confirmed.
